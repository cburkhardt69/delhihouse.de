Points to check with the accountant

    Possible duplicate ledgers and cost centres

    Found from the names: a misspelt word, or for party and staff accounts in the same group, one name contained in the other. Cases from other Tally exports are added by hand and show no posting count. Please confirm with the accountant before merging.

    Misspelt ledger and cost-centre names

    Words not in the dictionary that are one or two letters away from a known word. Names of people and firms are only compared with words used elsewhere in the books, so most proper names are not listed.
